Dammit, I need to be 18!

August 29th, 2008

God, people over 18 have all the fun.  But I’m not talking about that, you pervert. I’m talking of course, about Google Adsense. “No free money for you until you’re 18,” they say. F-berries. That really screws up my plans of becoming a millionaire off this crap, at least for 6 months. But hey, I need some regular traffic first. Once the traffic comes, the ads will start paying. Let’s hope, when I’m eligible for it, that it’ll at least pay $90/year, the price I pay for the site. Heck, the representative from iPower even said to my dad, “[Adam] should be making money off of this website”. Well, we’ll see if the interweb really likes all 300 of The Funny Guy Comix, after I finish digitizing them and upload them in cool Adobe PDF format for great success.
