FGComics News!

If you've come to this website by accident, all I can ask is how the heck you found us. FGComics is a website based on the comics that were drawn by Adam Young (me) in his 4th to 8th grades at Red Lion Area School District. FGComics.com also contains anything else I've made up to this point, mostly random stuff I can't quite find a place for on this great internet of ours...

Just maybe, something I put here could make me famous, or maybe something YOU would like put up could make... the both of us famous. ;)

  
 

Upgrade status...
We're getting closer to our intended point in the process here... I have most of the main, static site design implemented, next I'll fiddle with the colors for the forum, change it to a black color and add some profile forms, avatar size limits, the works. I have Funny Guy Comics 1-80 rescanned, which took about 2 hours of tedious work with that frustrating HP scanning stuff. They have much better quality, I scanned them in at 300dpi then resized them to a manageable size for the PDFs (~10MB total for each)
