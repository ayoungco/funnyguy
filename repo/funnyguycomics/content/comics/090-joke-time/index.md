Hey Kids! Before we close our 2nd book, I've got some jokes here! Q: Why did the hot dog win the race? A: Because he was the weiner! Q: How does the man in the moon cut his hair? A: Eclipse it! Q: What did the astronaut see in his frying pan? A: An unidentified frying object! Q: What disasters occur every 24 hours? A: Day breaks and night falls! Q: What does a raincloud wear under its coat? A: Thunderwear! See you in the 3rd book! The End
