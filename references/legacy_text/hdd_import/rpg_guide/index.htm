Thanks for downloading the FGRPG! This manual will tackle the most common mistakes, errors, and questions regarding use of the game, as well as give you a few tips on playing. Just follow the links below to the different pages of the manual.

A known fatal glitch in the game is related to the activation of the QuestMenu. DO NOT activate the QuestMenu with the number keys(0-9) while talking to a person, reading something, or while a cutscene is running. Doing so will result in the game experiencing an error that will close the application.
