Hi FGComics fans! :) I'm Element, the lead assigned Administrator of these discussion boards and the leader of the FGs. The forums are for FGComics discussion and anything else worth discussing! Here are the rules themselves, in all their glory and majesty.

[size=25][b]General Rules[/b][/size]

[list=1][*] No spamming topics. Spam includes, but is not limited to, the following:

[list][*] Short posts with little or no meaning, which are comments like "lol yea". Post why you said that short remark, not just that. Why did you rotfl? 
[*] Random posts that serve no purpose whatsoever, like “rfqwbriufbqouerbguqeqcer,” which makes me think you had a seizure on your keyboard.
[*] A post with an extremely large amount of emoticons.
[*] Advertising a commercial website, offering web hosting, etc.[/list]
[*] Do not “troll”. My definition of trolling is trying to force someone into an argument when they clearly don’t want one.
[*] Don’t double post. Double posting is posting consecutively in a topic. I don’t want to see one post that is acceptable, and a second one that says, “Oh my got I forget to tell you guys that …”. Edit your former post.
[*] Post in a way that passes as “normal” writing. Posts with lots of instant message jargon (lmao, lol, brb, g2g, etc.) are not normal. In addition, don’t post in a large text size or place an extremely large amount of emoticons in your message. Check your grammar and spelling as well, and make sure your post is legible with no decoding required.
[*] Give your threads appropriate names. Don’t put “hi” or “look here”. A more appropriate name would be “Does anyone like pizza?”.
[*] No flaming. Flaming is yelling at or unnecessarily criticizing other members. 
[*] Members are only allowed one account to themselves. If it is found that a user is using more than one account, the accounts will be dealt with according to the [i]administrator's judgment.[/i] Scary, huh?
[*] Don’t post exceedingly pornographic or sexually oriented material. This includes like to sites that have links to objectionable material, including an advertisement. Small, vaguely suggestive jokes are allowed, as long as they do not ridicule a specific person or group, or “educate” an 8-year-old.
[*] Don’t post if it’s not necessary. Have you ever heard the saying “If you haven’t got something nice to say, don’t say it at all”? That’s what this rule is based on. For example, say you really don’t like the game Tetris. Don’t go into a topic called “Tetris is kool!” and post “Tetris SUX!” 
[*] Post in the right forum. Read the forum description before you post in it. It gets really troublesome moving topics around to the right place to keep things organized 24/7.
[*] Respect your fellow members. Let’s create a thriving, happy community, a place where we can have healthy discussion without conflict.[/list]
