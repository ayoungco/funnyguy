              Contract

      It has briefly come to our attention over a smidgeon of hydrogen-
oxygen mix of slight ground light rind of lemon that
Repair Man is no longer a contestant worthy of opposing our savior in savvy
humorous strips, humor filled: Repair Man is now property of Funny Guy
Comix Corporation: Use of Repair Man logos, traits, or insignia is now
strictly prohibited
without consent of F.G. Comics Corporation. Those who sign this contract
agree with this action and thereby give permission to proceed with the
under taking of Mega Guy Comics.
