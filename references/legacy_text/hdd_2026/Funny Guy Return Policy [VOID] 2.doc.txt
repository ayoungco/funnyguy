
FGOAC Return Policy

      As our company grows bigger, we overtake certain responsibilities that
may require public attention. We have noticed the potential DANGER to our
handmade comic duplicates [copies] and it is vital for us to keep our
return policy in check. Any comics that are purchased TO READ ONLY have a
purchase right. If a comic is purchased for the reasonable 25 cents, and
the reader is not pleased with the strip they have just purchased, they may
return the comic in trade for their 25 cents. If, by any reason, the
purchased comic is damaged beyond repair, the buyer will NOT receive their
refund.
