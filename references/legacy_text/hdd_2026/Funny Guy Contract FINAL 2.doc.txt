      Prologue: This is the only contract contained within FGComics’
forums, website(s), comics’ reference, etcetera. Any and all others are
immediately void upon creation, even if one of the leaders (including the
president) signs it.

      I, the following, promise my heart to promoting the concept of Funny
Guy Comics Incorporated. I will do my best to help FGComics grow and
maintain its reputation. I understand that I must be active with my
