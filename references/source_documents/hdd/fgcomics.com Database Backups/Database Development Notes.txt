He had to constantly change the company's name to avoid lawsuits, but keep it generally the same so his aging grandmother, who raised him, could still send him letters.

Jack Audio "Jackie" his reluctant counterpart, is a cynical sound engineering guy that works for Auxy Moron and films his commercials and telethons.

Potty-P is a bartender from Funny Guy's hometown. They have been friends since they met early in the series, when Funny Guy impressed him with his eating skills. They have been at odds occasionally due to Funny Guy's occasional disregard for Potty-P's property. He runs a "Disco Club" that rarely hosts discos at all. One time he experimented with a "Spa" extension in an attempt to attract more customers, but his questionable menu was still enough to drive away everyone but Funny Guy, enjoying the oddly-named foods, which are actually quite tasty. 

Junior is the youngest of the Funny Guy brotherhood. Originally having a lisp and a problem with biting people, he has since overcome these issues. He is known to cause destruction when left unsupervised. He attends the Gas Town Elementary School. 

"Brother" was a comrade of the Funny Guy brothers during the Koven Wars. A man of colossal stature, he often was called upon by saying "Oh, Brother!" He would then show up to squish whoever was harassing the FGs.

Repair Man is a former enemy of the Funny Guys, minion of Koven, reduced to a minor annoyance in the FG universe. He often teams up with Serious Man when he is around to try and attack the Funny Guys, but otherwise he lives in trash cans and improvised housing. 

Koven is the second enemy of Funny Guy, deemed enough of a threat that the Evil Scientist even joined Funny Guy's side. His companion, the "monkey", is his adopted son. [Now has a mowhawk]
