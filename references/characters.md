# Character bible

Pulled from `../../funnyguycomics/content/history.md` and the comic
transcripts under `../../funnyguycomics/content/comics/`. Treat this as a
starting point, not exhaustive — read more transcripts before designing a
character that isn't listed here.

## Funny Guy

The title character. Originally a stick figure with dot eyes and wild,
undefined hair that fans call "Funny Guy Hair" — no one, including the
creator, has ever pinned down what it actually looks like in more detail
than that. Long-haired in the earliest strips. Keep the hair prominent and
weird in any redesign; it's the character's one non-negotiable trait.

Reference art: `../../funnyguycomics/static/comics/001_the_funny_guy.png`
and early strips in that directory for the original crude style.

## Koven

Funny Guy's original nemesis (based on a real kid from the creator's 4th
grade class, name changed). Central to the "Koven Wars" story arc. Later
largely retired as an active antagonist but referenced as historical
canon.

## Repair Man

Recurring character, introduced as a joke/butt-of-every-joke character
before being made "official" by in-universe contract. Distinct from the
unrelated "Repair Man Man Man Man Man Man Man Comics" character used by a
rival kid-comic (not part of Funny Guy canon).

## Serious Man

Villain introduced in 5th grade era strips as an antagonist for Funny Guy
after Koven left the class.

## Visual eras to be aware of

1. **Original stick-figure comics (2000–2003)** — pencil-on-notebook-paper,
   crude, six-panel grids, Captain Underpants-influenced.
2. **RPG Maker sprite art** — see `../../funnyguyrpg/CharSet/`,
   `FaceSet/`, and `Battle/` for how the cast was later translated into
   16-bit RPG sprites. Note `fg2.png` and `FG-GrayCharas.png` in `CharSet/`
   and `fgs.png` in `FaceSet/` as the closest existing "canonical" sprite
   renditions of the cast.
3. **Legacy raw art dump** — `/mnt/creative/funnyguy` has PSDs and
   scans spanning both eras plus unreleased/experimental art. Worth
   searching before assuming something doesn't have prior art.

Don't blend these eras by accident — decide which one a generation task is
targeting (see `style.md`) and stay consistent within a batch.
